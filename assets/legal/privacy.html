Privacy Policy

Cluckfall Heights · Last updated 26 August 2026

Short version: Cluckfall Heights does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data, and everything you create stays on your device. The one exception is a single, one-time signal sent to our attribution partner AppsFlyer at install, telling us whether the install came from a tracked marketing link or not. No accounts, no advertising, no profile of you.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the Cluckfall Heights mobile application handles information. It applies to the app itself, which runs entirely on your device.

2. Data we collect

The app has no sign-up, no login and no user identifiers, and it does not build a profile of you. It uses one attribution service, AppsFlyer, solely to learn whether an install came from a tracked marketing link ("non-organic") or not ("organic"). This is a one-time signal per install, not an ongoing analytics or advertising library: the app does not log what you do inside it, does not show ads, and does not share your storage plans, objects or profile photo with AppsFlyer or anyone else.

3. Data the app stores on your device

To do its job, the app saves the following locally, in its own private storage area:

  • Storage structures you create, including their type, dimensions and levels.
  • Objects you place, together with their width, depth, height, approximate weight and fragility.
  • Custom object profiles you define.
  • Your display name and profile photo, if you choose to add them.
  • App preferences such as measurement units, sound and haptics.
  • Whether you turned on the optional daily reminder, and the time you chose for it.

This information is never uploaded. It is not readable by other apps. Deleting the app removes it.

4. Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera or photo library access only at the moment you choose to set a profile photo, and only for that purpose. The photo you pick is cropped on device and saved into the app's private storage. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is not shared with any third party. You can remove it at any time from the profile screen, and you can revoke the permission in the system settings.

5. Notifications

Settings offers a single, optional daily reminder that nudges you to check your stored plans. It is off by default and only starts once you turn it on and pick a time. This reminder is scheduled entirely on your device using the operating system's local notification tools; it is not a push notification, no message is sent from any server, and it has no connection to install attribution or any other service the app uses. Turning it off, or denying the notification permission the OS will ask for, simply means it never fires.

6. Network access

Cluckfall Heights needs no connection to plan a structure, check its stability, or do anything else the app is for: every feature works fully offline, including the daily reminder above. The app contacts no servers of ours, because there are none. Shortly after each install, the app reports install attribution to AppsFlyer in the background, so we can tell how people find the app; this does not require anything you type or place inside the app, and it never repeats beyond that first report. Separately, if you deliberately tap a link that opens this policy or the support page in your browser, that also goes online, though both pages are also bundled inside the app so they can be read without a connection.

7. Children

The app is a general-purpose organisation tool. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no social features.

8. Your control over your data

Because everything is local, you are in full control. You can delete any structure or object inside the app, export your data to a file you own, remove your profile photo, turn off the daily reminder at any time, or uninstall the app to erase everything it stored.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new release of the app and will be published at the same web address. The date at the top of this page always shows the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the support page included in the app, or at cluckfallheights.com/support.html.

This copy is bundled inside the app so it remains readable without an internet connection.
