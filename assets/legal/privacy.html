Privacy Policy

Cluckfall Heights · Last updated 25 August 2026

Short version: Cluckfall Heights does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you create stays on your device.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the Cluckfall Heights mobile application handles information. It applies to the app itself, which runs entirely on your device.

2. Data we collect

None. The app has no sign-up, no login and no user identifiers. It does not contain analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries, and it does not build a profile of you.

3. Data the app stores on your device

To do its job, the app saves the following locally, in its own private storage area:

  • Storage structures you create, including their type, dimensions and levels.
  • Objects you place, together with their width, depth, height, approximate weight and fragility.
  • Custom object profiles you define.
  • Your display name and profile photo, if you choose to add them.
  • App preferences such as measurement units, sound and haptics.

This information is never uploaded. It is not readable by other apps. Deleting the app removes it.

4. Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera or photo library access only at the moment you choose to set a profile photo, and only for that purpose. The photo you pick is cropped on device and saved into the app's private storage. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is not shared with any third party. You can remove it at any time from the profile screen, and you can revoke the permission in the system settings.

5. Network access

Cluckfall Heights works fully offline and needs no connection to function. The app contacts no servers of ours, because there are none. The only case where your device may go online is if you deliberately tap a link that opens this policy or the support page in your browser, and those pages are also bundled inside the app so they can be read without a connection.

6. Children

The app is a general-purpose organisation tool. It collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no social features.

7. Your control over your data

Because everything is local, you are in full control. You can delete any structure or object inside the app, export your data to a file you own, remove your profile photo, or uninstall the app to erase everything it stored.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new release of the app and will be published at the same web address. The date at the top of this page always shows the current version.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the support page included in the app, or at cluckfallheights.com/support.html.

This copy is bundled inside the app so it remains readable without an internet connection.
