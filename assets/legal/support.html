Support

Cluckfall Heights · Last updated 25 August 2026

Cluckfall Heights runs entirely on your device. There is nothing to sign in to, and no connection is required, so most questions are about how the planner and the analysis work.

Getting started

Create a structure, choose whether it is a shelf, a cabinet, a storage box or a custom build, set its width, depth and height, then add the levels you need. Open the object library, place items on the levels, and the app recalculates the layout as you go.

How the stability status is decided

Every layout gets one of three states. Stable means the weight is spread reasonably and nothing fragile is at risk. Caution means something deserves a second look, for example a top-heavy upper section or one level carrying most of the load. Unstable means a clear problem was found, such as heavy items concentrated high up, a strongly offset centre of mass, or a fragile item sitting under something heavy.

The result is an approximation based on the dimensions, weights and fragility you enter. It is a planning aid, not an engineering certification, so always use your own judgement for anything that matters.

Why the centre of mass marker moves

The marker shows roughly where the combined weight of everything you placed is centred. Moving a heavy item to a lower level pulls it down, and moving it to one side pushes it sideways. The further it drifts from the middle of the base, the less stable the layout is.

Weight distribution

The distribution view compares how much load each level carries. It highlights the heaviest level, the imbalance between the upper and lower halves, and any level where heavy objects are bunched together.

Rearrangement suggestions

Suggestions appear only when they would genuinely help, for example when the status turns to Caution or Unstable, when a level is clearly overloaded, or when a fragile item is at risk. When a layout is already stable, the app stays quiet. You decide whether to accept a suggestion; nothing is moved without you.

Marking fragile items

Set the fragility level when you create or edit an object. Fragile items are marked in the layout, and the app warns you if one ends up under a heavy object, on an overloaded level, or in an unbalanced area.

Units of measurement

Centimetres with kilograms, or inches with pounds. Switch at any time in Settings; existing structures are converted for display and nothing is lost.

Your data

Structures, objects, custom profiles and preferences are stored only on this device. Settings includes export, which writes your data to a file you control, and import, which reads such a file back. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Profile photo

A profile photo is optional. If you add one, the app asks for camera or photo library access at that moment, crops the image on device and keeps it in its own private storage. You can replace or remove it any time from the profile screen.

Something not working

If a structure will not open, an analysis looks wrong, or the app behaves unexpectedly, please describe what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, and include your app version from Settings. Write to us at cluckfallheights.com/support.html.
